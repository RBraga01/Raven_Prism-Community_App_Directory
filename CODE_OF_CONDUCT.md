# Community conduct

This directory welcomes developers, creators and readers who wish to share
public Raven Prism experiences.

Contributions should be factual, respectful and relevant. Credit original
creators, discuss technical disagreements constructively, and do not submit
harassment, discriminatory content, threats, impersonation, spam or personal
information about others without consent.

Avoid posting credentials, private communications or vulnerability details in
public threads. Use GitHub's report/block functionality for abusive conduct.
For directory security disclosures follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit, reject, lock or remove content that violates these
rules and can restrict repeated abuse. Moderation decisions may be appealed
through a respectful follow-up via the repository's available private
contact options. Maintainer availability and response times are not guaranteed.
