What is Raven Prism?

Raven Prism is a standalone pair of smart glasses running Linux-based Raven OS. Its full-colour display is designed for information at a glance, with eye-controlled interaction. Developers can build apps with the Python-based Raven Framework and preview them in its simulator. Raven developer resources ↗
